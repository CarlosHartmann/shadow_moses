This step audits users who fall into groups that cannot both be true, and records how each case was decided.

Scripts
- overlap_audit.py: finds the contradictory overlaps (users from 2012 on, as in step 4) and writes overlap_audit_check.xlsx (sheets Summary, Users, Evidence). overlap_audit.xlsx holds the decisions (sheets Decisions, Age report, Gender report) and is never overwritten by the script; a clean check run shows no users in the Users sheet.
- derive_age_decisions.py: derives the rule-based age decisions (rules below) from the flair-derived birth year ranges in ../3a-grouping/grouped_subreddit_data/age.jsonl and rebuilds the age rows of the Decisions sheet and the Age report sheet of overlap_audit.xlsx (backup in backup/ first). Decisions made by hand, deletions and the political/gender rows are kept as they are.
- apply_decisions.py: rebuilds two override sheets in ../3a-grouping/grouped_flairs.xlsx from the Decisions and Gender report sheets of overlap_audit.xlsx (backup in ../3a-grouping/annotation_backup first). Run it after editing the decisions, then re-run 3a, 3b, 4 and onward.
- Order after a change upstream: 3a grouping.py, derive_age_decisions.py, apply_decisions.py, then 3b, overlap_audit.py (check), 4 and onward. (3a only reads the gender, political and age-delete overrides, which derive_age_decisions.py does not change; if those decisions are edited, re-run 3a before 3b.)

Contradictory overlaps (not tolerated)
- different generations; old and young; old with millennial/Z; young with silent/boomer/X
- male and female
- political conservative and progressive
- gender-political conservative and progressive (none found)
Overlaps like age + gender are possible for one person and are fine.

Why the overlaps happen
- Age: the 3b split is correct per comment, but a user's flairs imply different birth year ranges over time (updated flairs, stale flairs, wide ranges near a cutoff).
- Gender and political: flair annotation or genuinely changing users.

Principle: every decision applies only to the user named, never to other users sharing a flair. Flair annotations in grouped_flairs.xlsx are therefore left untouched; decisions are stored per user (user_group_overrides sheet).

Decisions: age (871 users, plus -caturday-night- decided by hand as 1979)
- Rule: take the highest lo and the lowest hi of all birth year ranges derived from the user's flairs and posting years (age comments from 2012 on).
- 467 users: common span lies inside one group. Recorded as "birth year(s) ..." in Decisions.
- 99 users: common span crosses a group boundary. Same span recorded; the majority bucket (existing tie-break, older bucket on ties) then decides.
- 305 users had no common year because flairs went stale (e.g. "16" kept for years). For these the range of each flair's first appearance is used instead. This resolved 198 users.
- 107 users had no common year even by first appearance: marked "Needs decision" in Decisions (flairs and ranges in columns D/E) and then decided as follows.
- 85 young users (teenagers/young adults, flair-derived ranges all within 1995-1999) were set to the average of the midpoints of all their ranges.
- 14 users were given a birth year (range) by hand, among them three older users (granny_knows_best, theyearofthe_rat, zzsjourney).
- 8 users were set to "delete" by hand: actamis, afterburneranon, nooblevitamins, cooldude_4000, hey_laaady, notjimirsay, snowblindalbino, thisini. They are removed from the age group (3a) and from the baseline (4).
- Re-derived on 2026-10-17 with derive_age_decisions.py, after flairs on comments before 2015 began to be counted from 2015 (see ../3b-age-grouping/notes.txt): only 417 users still had contradictory age comments, since many earlier contradictions (e.g. "16" kept for years) came from counting stale-looking flairs from the year of the comment. 509 earlier rule-based decisions were dropped as no longer needed. Now: 282 intersection, 96 crossing a boundary, 14 first appearance, 13 average of the midpoints (3 as before, plus 10 young users who had no common year any more: boot_man, choppps, gamerlefay, im_clueless_its_true, kingsleythewizard, nazoropaz, saget84, shakeitandbakeit, thefacter, thetruthliar), 13 decided by hand and kept (alonelyrubberband and nsa-surveillance among them, although no longer contradictory), and the 8 deletions. -tommy, decided by hand as 1996 from the old ranges, now follows the rules (1997). "Born in the 1960s" and "Born late 1960s" had been annotated as the single year 1960 and were corrected to the ranges 1960-1969 and 1965-1969, which resolved trelene.
- Decisions rows starting with "birth year" go to the age_user_overrides sheet; ../3b-age-grouping/deduce_birthyear.py then uses that range for all of the user's comments instead of the flair-derived ones.

Why users were deleted (age and gender)
- Users were removed from the age and gender groups (and from the baseline) when their flairs suggested that they had lied in non-minor ways, or, for gender, had transitioned within the data timespan. Minor inconsistencies were resolved with the rules above instead.
