Title: We Could Be A Masterpiece
Content: ```For years I've painted outside the lines sketched for me, + outside the canvas onto the frame. Every memory is a stroke of bristles— some sharp + rough, while others are smooth + blend nicely. Some are monotone greys + blacks. Others are vivid violets, ostentatious oranges, + colours only the mantis shrimp can perceive. Some strokes are faded or covered over. Others are the basis around which other colours are matched to or tainted by.

With you, we had our own canvas on its own frame. Comforting colours dazzled the frame, though there were some greys here + there. Abstract feelings of the grass between our toes in the field, sunshine bathing our skin, scratchy jazz from a record player, apple cider…

But you took a knife to the canvas.

And as the great Pablo Picasso once said, “Art is the elimination of the unnecessary”.

I can mend the canvas with your help. Take tweezers + restore the art with a precise hand, some canvas fibers + glue creating a neat seam. It’ll leave a scar, but we can wait for the glue to dry + slowly begin painting it over again...

Or I can rip apart the canvas we created. The knife hole is small, but you made it bigger. I can tear it apart as I please, because I have a canvas of my own with gorgeous colours you will never have the chance to behold. I hold both paint brushes + the knife in my hands now because you lost the privileges to it. I can eliminate you from the process if you prove yourself unnecessary + incapable of change.

The choice is yours.```
